Third round: Outlook froze periodically because each 5-minute cycle re-read the whole calendar (about 90 s) and retried 17 encrypted messages Outlook cannot decrypt. Now an unchanged calendar fingerprint skips the calendar read, and an unreadable item is retried only after it changes. Still freezing: a quiet cycle listed 21,116 rows of 33 folders row by row, 37 s of calls; now batched (1.8 s) and skipped for folders whose fingerprint is unchanged (0.3 s for all 33) (`OutlookGraph.AnUnchangedFolderIsNotListedAgain`, `OutlookGraph.AnUnchangedCalendarIsNotReadAgain`, `OutlookGraph.AnUnreadableItemIsTriedAgainOnlyOnceItChanges`)
