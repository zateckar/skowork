# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Meeting Preparation Presentation
The answer to "Ask Skowork" on a meeting SHALL be presented with the meeting_prep Content template (client/work-inbox Content Templates): the meeting's key facts, what it is about, what to prepare or bring as a task list, and background with Citations.

#### Scenario: Prepare for a meeting
- **WHEN** the user chooses "Ask Skowork" on tomorrow's steering meeting
- **THEN** the Thread shows a meeting_prep surface with time, organizer and attendees, the topic, a preparation list and cited background
