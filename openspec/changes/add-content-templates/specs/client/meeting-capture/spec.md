# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Meeting Summary Presentation
The meeting summary SHALL be presented with the meeting_summary Content template (client/work-inbox Content Templates): the meeting's key facts, the summary, the decisions, and the follow-ups as a task list with owner and due date where the meeting named them, in the meeting's language.

#### Scenario: Follow-ups with owners
- **WHEN** a meeting agreed that Klára checks the order balance by 2 December
- **THEN** the summary's follow-ups list that task with Klára as owner and 2 December as due date
